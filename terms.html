Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Purpose

SUZZY SNS Analytics is a personal, experimental tool intended to organize analytics from a TikTok account authorized by its owner. The integration is under development and may be unavailable.

2. Eligibility and authorization

Only the owner of, or a person explicitly authorized to access, a TikTok account may connect that account. Users must follow TikTok's applicable terms and policies.

3. Permitted use

Any enabled functionality may only be used for legitimate analytics and content planning. The tool does not grant ownership of TikTok data or permission to access another person's account.

4. Availability and accuracy

The tool is provided as-is during development. Availability and metrics depend on data supplied through authorized APIs and may be incomplete or delayed. No performance result is guaranteed.

5. Changes and termination

Features and terms may change. Account access may be discontinued at any time, including when authorization is revoked.

6. Contact

For general inquiries, use the project's GitHub Issues page. Do not post passwords, access tokens, or private information in public issues.
